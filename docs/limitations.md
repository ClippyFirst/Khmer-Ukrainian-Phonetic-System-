# Limitations

This repository is a **research foundation**, not yet a complete empirically validated Khmer → Ukrainian transcription standard.

## Established or strongly supported scope

- Khmer Unicode/orthographic structure is modelled conservatively.
- Two consonant classes/registers and their role in vowel interpretation are represented.
- The research pipeline distinguishes orthography, phonology, phonetics/IPA and Ukrainian practical output.
- Russian practical transcription is treated as comparative evidence rather than as a Ukrainian norm.

## NOT ESTABLISHED

- a complete lexical pronunciation dictionary;
- a complete orthography-to-IPA engine for every vowel sign combination;
- a corpus-derived ranking model for Ukrainian candidates;
- an adjudicated benchmark of Ukrainian outputs;
- universal narrow IPA for every lexical item;
- a normative Ukrainian standard for Khmer names.

## EVIDENCE LIMITED

- rare/obsolete Khmer signs;
- loanword-specific pronunciations;
- all dialect-specific variants;
- connected-speech processes;
- a standalone Serbian Khmer practical transcription standard;
- a Bulgarian Cyrillic Khmer practical transcription standard.

The system therefore returns explicit unsupported/uncertain states rather than inventing pronunciations.
