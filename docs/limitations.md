# Limitations

This foundation deliberately does not claim that the full Khmer phonological system has been solved.

## NOT ESTABLISHED
- complete lexical pronunciation coverage
- corpus-attested syllable inventory
- complete context-sensitive phonological rule inventory
- optimal Ukrainian candidate ranking weights
- full Ukrainian orthographic realization for every foreign phonological sequence
- narrow acoustic IPA for every word

## EVIDENCE LIMITED
- exhaustive modern treatment of rare/historical Khmer signs
- all dialect-specific pronunciations
- loanword-specific pronunciation rules
- connected-speech processes

These gaps are tracked rather than filled with guesses.
